Tossel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Tossel is a personal app for tracking university courses, graded items and GPA. This policy explains what happens to your data.

Summary

Tossel does not collect, transmit or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

Data you enter

Courses, grades, dates, credits and your profile name are stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. They are never sent to the developer or to any server, and the app has no accounts.

Syllabus import and the assistant

When you import a syllabus (PDF or screenshots) or use the assistant chat, the text is read and understood on your device, using Apple's on-device frameworks (PDFKit, Vision and, where available, Apple Intelligence). Your documents, images and messages are not uploaded anywhere by Tossel.

Tracking and analytics

Tossel contains no advertising, no analytics, no third-party code and no tracking.

Beta testing

If you install Tossel through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to send with the developer, according to Apple's own terms and privacy policy. Tossel itself adds nothing to this.

Children

Tossel does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
